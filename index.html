Where it sits

Coding agents already read your KiCad project, pull the BOM and compare distributors. They stop before the part that costs money. Partsgate is that last step, not another search engine: one MCP server with six tools, company accounts, per-agent spend mandates and a human approval queue.

What stays under your control

A mandate per agent
Maximum per order, monthly cap, allowed categories and an approval threshold. Hard limits are denied before anything is sent.
A human above the threshold
Over the threshold the order waits. Nothing reaches the distributor until an approver says yes. A rejection needs a reason.
Re-quote before placing
The price and stock are checked again right before the order goes out, and again on approval. If they changed, the order stops.
Confirmed means confirmed
An order is confirmed only when the distributor returns its own order number. What an agent claims about payment or status is ignored.

Run it yourself

Partsgate is open source (Apache-2.0) and self-hosted first: your team runs it with your own distributor keys. See the self-host quickstart.
