Pricing

We do not know yet what teams will pay, so we are not pretending to. Here is what is true today.

Design partner

EUR 0

For the first companies, while we learn. You get setup help and a direct line to the builder.

  • Agents, mandates and the approval queue
  • Mouser first, DigiKey when it is verified
  • Low caps on real orders to start

In return: a short call every two weeks and honest feedback on what blocked you.

After the pilot

Flat, per company

One monthly price per company, not per agent and not per part. The number is not set yet, and design partners hear it before it applies.

  • No percentage on your parts
  • You pay the distributor on your own account

An optional per-order fee is being considered. Nothing is decided.

What we do not do

  • We do not resell parts or add a margin. The order is placed on your own distributor account at your own prices.
  • We do not hold your money. Payment is the distributor's account billing.
  • We do not publish a price we have not tested with real buyers.

Become a design partner

You fit if a hardware team of one to fifty reorders parts regularly and you would let an agent do it inside a cap. Write to the address on the sign-up page once it exists. For now this site is a placeholder and has no contact form.

Partsgate is a working name. Domain, company and pricing are placeholders (partsgate.example).
