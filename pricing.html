Pricing

Partsgate is open source and self-hosted first. We do not know yet what teams will pay for extras, so we are not pretending to.

Open source

EUR 0

Apache-2.0. You run it, with your own distributor keys, as your own team's purchasing tool.

  • Agents, mandates and the approval queue
  • Mouser and DigiKey connectors, not yet verified live
  • Your keys and your distributor account stay with you

See the self-host quickstart.

Possible extras

Not decided

Hypothesis, unverified: paid modules for approvals, audit trail, single sign-on and multiple sites, plus support. Hosted only where a distributor approves it in writing.

  • No percentage on your parts
  • You pay the distributor on your own account

Nothing here is for sale yet.

Why self-hosted

Distributor API terms tie their credentials to an internal application used by the account holder. A shared gateway that orders on many customers' accounts would need each distributor's written approval, so the supported path is that every team runs its own copy.

What we do not do

  • We do not resell parts or add a margin. The order is placed on your own distributor account at your own prices.
  • We do not hold your money. Payment is the distributor's account billing.
  • We do not publish a price we have not tested with real buyers.

Early adopters

You fit if a hardware team of one to fifty reorders parts regularly and you would let an agent do it inside a cap. We help the first teams set it up. Write to the address on the sign-up page once it exists. For now this site is a placeholder and has no contact form.

Partsgate is a working name. Domain, company and pricing are placeholders (partsgate.example).
